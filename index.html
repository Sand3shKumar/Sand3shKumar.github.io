Our story

Started with a project.
Built into a company.

We are two Computer Science students who started Infresco in our 3rd semester — not after graduation, not after years of experience. Right then, in the middle of university.

Our OOP course gave us a Java project. We added MySQL, built a full management system, and presented it to our teacher. They were amazed. They presented our project to the entire class as an example of what was possible.

Over tea after that presentation, we looked at what we had built and made a decision: this is worth more than a grade. Infresco was born that afternoon.

Three months later — two complete ERP systems running in real businesses. A third in active development. And our first major multi-business client signed on the day of the first pitch.

We are young. We work harder because of it, not in spite of it. Every client gets our full attention — not a junior account manager, not a ticket system. Us. Directly.
