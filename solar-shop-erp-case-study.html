The business and the challenge

Veer Electronic is a solar equipment shop based in Tando Jam, Sindh, Pakistan — selling panels, batteries, inverters, and complete solar systems. In just over a year of operation the business had grown to the point where manual management was becoming a serious problem.

Like most small businesses in Pakistan, Veer Electronic was managing everything on paper registers. Every sale was handwritten. Every stock entry was manual. Every credit record lived in a notebook. And every morning started with the same tedious process of going through those records to understand where the business stood.

"Managing low quantity items and getting a record of each item, sales, and credit records was the biggest challenge. Most of the work was done manually."

— Kelash Kumar, Owner, Veer Electronic

What we built

Infresco built a complete solar shop management system from scratch — designed specifically around how Veer Electronic operates on a daily basis. The goal was simple: replace every paper register and manual process with one fast, reliable digital system.

Invoice Generation

The most used feature every day. Before the system, every invoice was handwritten — time consuming, error prone, and impossible to track. Now every sale generates a complete digital invoice instantly with all product details, quantities, prices, and customer information. What used to take minutes now takes seconds.

Stock and Inventory Management

Managing solar equipment inventory — panels, batteries, inverters, and complete systems — requires tracking multiple product types with different quantities and price points. The system maintains a complete real-time stock register for all products and sends automatic alerts when any item falls below the minimum stock level.

Credit Records and Customer Accounts

One of the most painful parts of running a shop in Pakistan is tracking which customers have outstanding balances. The system maintains a complete digital ledger for every customer — all transactions, payments made, and current balance. The owner gets automatic daily alerts about customer accounts so nothing falls through the cracks.

Data Backup System

All business data is backed up automatically. No more fear of losing records if something goes wrong. Every transaction, every invoice, every stock entry — all safely stored and recoverable.

Unique Feature
The Black Box System
One of the most unique features Infresco built for Veer Electronic is what the owner calls the "black box system" — a complete audit trail that records every action taken inside the system. Every login. Every change. Every deletion. The system logs who did what, when, and from which account.
Records every login with timestamp and user identity
Tracks every change made to stock or invoices
Identifies exactly who made any modification
Cannot be deleted or modified — permanent record
This feature gives the business owner complete accountability over everything that happens in the system. If anything goes wrong — the black box shows exactly what happened, who did it, and when. It is the kind of feature that large enterprise software charges a premium for. Infresco built it for a small shop in Tando Jam.

What made this project different

The black box system is what stands out from this project. It was not in the original requirements — it emerged from a conversation about accountability and trust in a small business environment.

In Pakistani small businesses, the owner cannot always be present. Staff manage the system in their absence. The black box gives the owner complete visibility into every action taken — even when they are not there. This is enterprise-level accountability built for a small shop.
